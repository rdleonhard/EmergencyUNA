# Counsel Engagement Authority

> **Drafting note:** This template is experimental and must be tailored by counsel. It is intended to authorize emergency counsel engagement without conceding that the DAO is a legal entity or that any participant is personally liable for DAO activity.

## Emergency counsel authority

Upon an Emergency Trigger, the DAO authorizes [Emergency Manager / multisig / committee / designated representative] to engage counsel for the limited purpose of responding to the emergency.

## Scope of permitted engagement

Counsel may be engaged to:

- evaluate legal notices, subpoenas, complaints, investigations, arbitration demands, or other claims;
- appear specially or otherwise preserve procedural objections;
- contest service, jurisdiction, venue, standing, capacity, association status, member status, agency, liability, or applicable law;
- communicate with courts, regulators, arbitrators, counterparties, service providers, or opposing counsel;
- coordinate document preservation and litigation holds;
- advise on privilege and confidentiality;
- advise on treasury use for legal-defense purposes; and
- take other actions reasonably necessary to preserve rights during the emergency.

## Limits on authority

Unless separately approved through the DAO's applicable governance process, counsel is not authorized to:

- admit liability;
- settle or compromise claims;
- waive substantive defenses;
- concede that the DAO is an unincorporated nonprofit association or other legal entity;
- concede that any token holder, voter, delegate, multisig signer, developer, contributor, or service provider is a member, partner, agent, fiduciary, or representative;
- bind any participant personally;
- authorize protocol upgrades;
- transfer treasury assets beyond approved legal-defense amounts; or
- make public statements beyond procedural or defensive communications.

## Budget

The initial emergency legal-defense budget is [amount / token amount / stablecoin amount], subject to the following controls:

- payments must be approved by [multisig / vote / committee];
- counsel invoices should be reviewed by [role];
- privileged descriptions should not be publicly disclosed; and
- public reporting should disclose aggregate spend where appropriate without waiving privilege.

## No admission

This authorization is made solely for emergency legal-defense purposes. It is not an admission that the DAO is an unincorporated nonprofit association or other legal entity, and it does not create personal obligations for token holders, voters, delegates, multisig signers, developers, contributors, or other participants.
