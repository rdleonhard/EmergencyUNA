# Emergency Manager Appointment

> **Drafting note:** This template is experimental. It should be reviewed by counsel before any use. Bracketed terms must be tailored to the DAO, jurisdiction, governance system, and emergency facts.

## Emergency appointment

Upon the occurrence of an Emergency Trigger, the DAO may appoint an emergency manager, agent, committee, or other authorized representative solely for the limited purposes described in this resolution.

**Emergency Trigger** means any of the following:

- receipt or attempted receipt of legal process, subpoena, investigation notice, enforcement notice, arbitration demand, demand letter, or comparable legal notice naming or purporting to bind the DAO;
- credible threat of litigation, regulatory action, treasury compromise, governance capture, or protocol-level emergency;
- inability of ordinary governance to act within the time required to preserve legal rights; or
- any other emergency approved by [governance threshold / multisig threshold / security council threshold].

## Appointed person

The DAO appoints [name / wallet / entity / committee] as Emergency Manager for the limited term and scope set forth below.

## Scope of authority

The Emergency Manager may:

1. receive and preserve legal notices without conceding the validity of service;
2. coordinate with counsel regarding emergency legal response;
3. retain counsel if separately authorized under the DAO's emergency counsel process;
4. communicate procedural positions to courts, regulators, arbitrators, counterparties, or service providers;
5. preserve records and coordinate litigation holds;
6. request emergency treasury authorization within approved limits; and
7. report to DAO governance regarding emergency actions taken.

The Emergency Manager may not, unless separately authorized:

- admit liability;
- settle claims;
- waive jurisdictional, procedural, merits, or status defenses;
- bind token holders, voters, delegates, contributors, developers, multisig signers, or other participants personally;
- upgrade or alter protocol contracts;
- transfer treasury assets beyond approved emergency limits;
- issue, burn, mint, or modify tokens;
- amend governance documents; or
- convert the DAO into a legal entity.

## Term

The appointment begins on [effective date / block / governance vote] and expires on the earliest of:

- [number] days after appointment;
- conclusion of the emergency;
- revocation by DAO governance;
- appointment of a successor;
- court order; or
- completion of the limited purpose for which authority was granted.

## Reporting

The Emergency Manager must provide a public or governance-restricted report, as appropriate, describing:

- actions taken;
- counsel retained;
- funds requested or spent;
- notices received;
- deadlines identified;
- unresolved risks; and
- recommendations for ratification, extension, or termination.

## No admission

This appointment is made without admission that the DAO is an unincorporated nonprofit association or any other legal entity, and without admission that the appointed person is a general manager, officer, partner, fiduciary, or agent for any purpose other than the limited emergency authority expressly granted.
