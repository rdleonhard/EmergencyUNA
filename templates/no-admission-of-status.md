# No-Admission Fallback Statement

> **Drafting note:** This template is experimental and must be tailored by counsel. It is designed for a DAO that does not intend to concede that it is an unincorporated nonprofit association or any other legal entity merely by adopting emergency procedures.

## Resolution

The DAO does not admit, concede, represent, or agree that it is an unincorporated nonprofit association, general partnership, joint venture, legal entity, or association of persons under the law of any jurisdiction.

The DAO adopts this emergency fallback statement solely to preserve optionality and continuity if a court, regulator, counterparty, arbitral body, administrative agency, or other competent authority asserts that the DAO is an unincorporated nonprofit association or analogous legal arrangement.

## No waiver

Nothing in this statement waives any argument, defense, objection, immunity, limitation, or position available to the DAO or to any participant, including any argument concerning:

- personal jurisdiction;
- subject-matter jurisdiction;
- service of process;
- venue;
- standing;
- capacity;
- association status;
- member status;
- agency;
- liability;
- enforceability;
- due process; or
- applicable law.

## Limited purpose

This statement may be used only for emergency legal-defense, service-response, governance-continuity, or counsel-retention purposes. It does not authorize ordinary protocol governance, treasury deployment beyond approved emergency limits, smart-contract upgrades, token issuance, token burns, settlement of claims, or admission of liability unless separately approved through the DAO's applicable governance process.

## Participant protection

Publication, voting, discussion, delegation, abstention, token ownership, multisig participation, contribution, or other interaction with this statement should not be interpreted as an admission by any person that such person is a member, partner, agent, fiduciary, manager, officer, representative, or controlling person of the DAO.
