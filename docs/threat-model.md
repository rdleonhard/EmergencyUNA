# Threat Model

EmergencyUNA assumes the DAO is facing a legal or operational emergency in which the absence of a conventional legal wrapper, registered agent, board, officer, or manager could make response difficult.

This document identifies scenarios the templates should address.

## Scenario 1: Service of process

A regulator, plaintiff, or counterparty attempts to serve the DAO through a forum post, governance interface, help desk, front-end operator, developer, token holder, multisig signer, Discord moderator, or other visible participant.

Key risks:

- no one has clear authority to accept or reject service;
- deadlines begin running before the DAO coordinates a response;
- a default judgment or adverse order is entered;
- individual participants are pressured to respond personally; and
- privileged communications are mishandled.

Emergency response goals:

- identify who may receive legal notices;
- preserve objections to improper service;
- authorize counsel to appear without conceding merits or entity status;
- notify governance participants through authenticated channels; and
- create a record of limited emergency authorization.

## Scenario 2: Regulatory enforcement

A regulator names the DAO, protocol, token holders, voters, developers, or other participants in an investigation, subpoena, complaint, administrative action, or settlement demand.

Key risks:

- inconsistent public statements;
- unmanaged document preservation duties;
- uncertainty over who can retain counsel;
- accidental waiver of privilege;
- treasury paralysis; and
- pressure on individual participants to settle or cooperate separately.

Emergency response goals:

- trigger a litigation-hold process;
- appoint counsel for the asserted association or emergency defense committee;
- separate DAO-wide defense from individual defenses;
- define settlement authority; and
- preserve the position that emergency response is not an admission of legal status.

## Scenario 3: Treasury compromise or sanctions risk

The DAO treasury is compromised, frozen, exploited, subject to sanctions risk, or otherwise unavailable when counsel, forensic support, or emergency vendors must be retained.

Key risks:

- inability to pay counsel or vendors;
- unauthorized treasury movement;
- disputes over emergency spending;
- sanctions, AML, or money-transmission issues; and
- governance-capture attempts during the emergency.

Emergency response goals:

- define emergency treasury caps;
- require multisig or on-chain execution where possible;
- document the legal basis for payments;
- require sanctions screening when appropriate; and
- provide post-emergency reporting.

## Scenario 4: Governance capture

A hostile actor accumulates voting power, compromises delegates, manipulates quorum, exploits voter apathy, or uses emergency rhetoric to centralize control.

Key risks:

- emergency procedures become a takeover mechanism;
- counsel is retained by the wrong faction;
- treasury assets are moved without legitimate authority;
- governance records become unreliable; and
- courts or counterparties receive conflicting authority claims.

Emergency response goals:

- require authenticated proposal channels;
- define quorum and approval thresholds;
- separate emergency legal authority from protocol-upgrade authority;
- create challenge and ratification procedures; and
- preserve evidence of the governance process.

## Scenario 5: Protocol shutdown or front-end failure

The DAO's normal governance tools are unavailable because of technical failure, censorship, domain seizure, hosting interruption, smart-contract exploit, or emergency upgrade.

Key risks:

- no reliable notice channel;
- inability to conduct an ordinary vote;
- fragmented community response;
- emergency actors exceed authority; and
- adversaries argue that the DAO cannot act.

Emergency response goals:

- identify fallback notice channels;
- define minimum authentication requirements;
- authorize temporary legal-defense action;
- require later ratification when normal governance returns; and
- keep emergency authority time-limited.
